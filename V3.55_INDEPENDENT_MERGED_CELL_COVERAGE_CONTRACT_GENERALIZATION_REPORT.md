## 6. Candidate behavior

The candidate generalized the intended responsibility split:

1. direct V3.51 proofs remain valid without coverage;
2. explicit merged coverage recovers only a sole `MODEL_ROW_OWNERSHIP_MISMATCH`;
3. explicit model-row and value-row membership is required;
4. document, table, model, parameter, column, value, conflict, and chunk boundaries remain
   exact;
5. coverage membership must be stable across page/chunk replication;
6. physical span must be valid but never selects or counts owned rows.

The frozen base reason remains in rejected records alongside the decisive coverage veto.
This keeps the original failure auditable and prevents coverage from concealing an
independent proof defect.
