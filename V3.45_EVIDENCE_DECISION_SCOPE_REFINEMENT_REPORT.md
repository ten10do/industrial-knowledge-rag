## 4. Implementation

`evidence-v345-decision-scope-candidate` composes the unchanged V3.42 candidate and adds
an `EvidenceDecisionScopeAction` with three outcomes:

- `UPGRADE`: preserve a safe V3.42 sufficiency upgrade;
- `VETO`: revoke an existing ANSWER only for a high-confidence explicit unsafe conflict;
- `PRESERVE`: retain every other V3.42 decision.

The candidate never converts a V3.42 ABSTAIN into ANSWER. It also never vetoes for missing
evidence or mere insufficiency. Explicit veto is limited to frozen relation-checker conflicts
such as cross-model leakage, manufacturer expansion, sibling-model borrowing, section or
reference mismatch, abbreviation conflict, negation, or a same-attribute competing
same-unit value. The veto confidence floor is 0.96.

This preserves the V3.29 boundary: Evidence decides sufficiency/citation/answerability but
does not generate an answer. It does not authorize family inheritance, manufacturer-level
expansion, parameter borrowing, or cross-model leakage. Retrieval, parser, Support,
semantic judge, NLI threshold, open-sufficiency base logic, and V3.41 Identity reasoning
were not modified.

## 7. Failure taxonomy

The nine inherited false answers did not expose a usable explicit-conflict signal to V3.45:

| V3.45 recheck class | Count | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| `ATTRIBUTE_NOT_BOUND` | 3 | no attribute-local conflict can be assessed |
| `ATTRIBUTE_RELATION_MISSING` | 4 | selected chunks do not bind the requested relation |
| relation reported supported despite qualifier/scope conflict | 2 | no unsafe relation is emitted |

The first seven cases are intentionally preserved because V3.45 forbids vetoing on
insufficiency or missing bindings. The remaining two demonstrate a relation-ownership gap:
the requested value occurs in retrieved evidence, but the factory-setting or section
qualifier is not represented by the frozen relation decision.

The ten false refusals remain upstream-heavy: 6 Identity-boundary, 3 missing-retrieval, and
1 Evidence/non-verification-path case. V3.45 neither owns nor changes these failures.

## 10. Next phase recommendation

Enter **Evidence Decision Claim-Binding Reassessment**, not Generation Evaluation.

The reassessment should determine whether the missing signal belongs to the Evidence
relation representation or to retrieval-to-Evidence claim selection. It should specifically
audit attribute vocabulary coverage, relation qualifiers such as “factory setting,” section
ownership, and whether an explicit competing value survives into the selected bounded
window. It must remain read-only over the frozen V3.45 artifacts before authorizing any new
candidate.

Do not modify Support, expand Identity, relax NLI, tune retrieval/parser, or advance to
independent/sealed evaluation until a bounded explicit-conflict representation is shown to
activate without unsafe vetoes.
