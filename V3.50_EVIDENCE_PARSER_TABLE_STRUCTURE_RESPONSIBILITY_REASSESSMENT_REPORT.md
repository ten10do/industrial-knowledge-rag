## 3. Reassessment scope and method

V3.50 analyzes ownership actions rather than treating every V3.49 error as a parser defect.
An ownership action is either:

1. a supported ownership binding (`ownership_relation != UNSUPPORTED`); or
2. a new V3.49 ownership veto (`action = VETO`).

The 15 action annotations were frozen before the reassessment result was generated. Each
annotation requires an exact query ID, action kind, observed correctness, failure class,
class-derived responsibility, required structure-proof fields, and a concrete reason. The
validator derives action type and correctness from the frozen candidate result rather than
trusting the annotation.

Final errors are classified with strict precedence:

1. an incorrect annotated ownership action;
2. registered evidence absent from selected candidates;
3. an inherited refusal from the frozen Evidence chain.

This precedence prevents a missing retrieved row from being attributed to parser structure,
and prevents inherited Evidence refusals from being attributed to the V3.49 ownership layer.

## 6. Minimum table-structure proof contract

V3.50 registers the minimum vocabulary that a future proof contract must be able to express.
The fields are conditional by relation; not every proof must populate every field.

| Proof group | Fields | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Region identity | `table_region_id`, `row_id`, `column_id` | Prove that model, parameter, and value occupy the same owned table region. |
| Header inheritance | `header_path`, `merged_cell_span`, `section_scope_id` | Preserve multi-level headers, merged cells, and explicit section inheritance. |
| Cell semantics | `cell_role`, `model_scope_id`, `parameter_scope_id`, `value_scope_id`, `qualifier_scope_id` | Keep model/attribute/value/default/action roles separate. |
| Reference and conflict | `reference_target`, `conflicting_owner_ids` | Bind explicit cross references and make competing owners auditable. |

The contract must be stable across chunk boundaries: splitting a table may duplicate proof
metadata, but it must not change row, column, header, or owner identity. The contract is
evidence provenance, not an answer. It must not encode a conclusion such as `ANSWER` or
identity compatibility.

## 7. Structure-producer responsibility

The structure-producing boundary owns extraction and preservation of physical table facts:

- table-region, row, and column identity;
- multi-level header paths and merged-cell spans;
- cell roles and source text belonging to each cell;
- explicit model, parameter, value, qualifier, and section scope anchors;
- cross-reference source/target linkage;
- conflicts when more than one row, column, model, or header could own a value.

V3.50 does not decide that the current parser alone must implement all fields. Parsing may
extract them, while chunking or ingestion metadata may preserve them. The responsibility is
assigned to a structure-producing contract upstream of Evidence. Exact component ownership
requires a separate design phase and an explicit unfreeze decision.

## 8. Evidence responsibility and safety

Evidence owns validation, not structure invention. A future Evidence consumer may accept a
proof only when:

1. target/model scope matches the resolved claim;
2. relation and parameter scope match;
3. value, qualifier, or action belongs to the proved row/column role;
4. header and section inheritance are explicit;
5. no conflicting owner exists.

Otherwise Evidence must return `UNSUPPORTED` and abstain. Identity compatibility must never
substitute for table ownership. Manufacturer-level expansion, family inheritance, parameter
borrowing, and cross-model leakage remain forbidden.

V3.50 introduces no runtime decision, relaxation, veto, or new prediction. Safety and
engineering checks:
