## 9. Gold Annotation Method

Prediction-blind BY CONSTRUCTION — reused the benchmark's own freeze-time claim layer instead
of annotating post hoc:

- ANSWERABLE cases (26) carry `claims[]` + `anchors[]` (document/page/quote/regex) written
  during V3.77 alignment from corpus evidence;
- `gold(SUPPORTED)` := claims non-empty AND an anchor matches retrieved chunk text
  (mechanical locality check — quote substring OR frozen regex);
- `gold(UNSUPPORTED)` := claims empty (43 abstain-gold cases);
- `gold(AMBIGUOUS)` := claims non-empty but no anchored span among retrieved chunks.

This satisfies §40 (no runtime-outcome-informed labels): the claim layer predates and is
independent of runtime behavior.

## 33. Engineering Decision

The feasibility question is answered precisely rather than optimistically. A claim-support
signal DOES exist inside the repo (support-v316.1's structured requirement coverage), it IS
deterministic, cheap, gold-independent, and it WOULD have caught 8/9 FAs including the entire
contract-acceptance bypass family — but its discrimination power and its failure mode are the
same thing: requirement-extraction blindness on generically-phrased queries yields AMBIGUOUS
verdicts that are evenly polluted by 6 FAs and 7 correct answers, and no pre-registerable
aggregation rule can split them (strictness buys blocks at exactly the price of kills;
definiteness preserves answers but forfeits the contract path entirely). The local NLI — tested
on its actual distinct task — provides no help whatsoever. What would change the outcome is NOT
calibration but a coverage upgrade: making the requirement extractor read definitional/generic
question forms (new structured capability, own feasibility risk) or introducing an entity-scoped
fact lattice (new architecture). Both exceed V3.79 scope. Per §54 the honest terminal state is
PARTIAL with STOP — no threshold edits, no gate wiring, no flag fabrication.
