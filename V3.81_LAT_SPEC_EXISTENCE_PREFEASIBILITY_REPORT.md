Two structural roots dominate BOTH failure classes and the measured blind spots:
(1) flattened tables destroy column semantics — the same root that zeroed PARAM_ROW;
(2) single-window keyword binding cannot attribute value↔family when several nouns share a line.
Both require layout-aware parsing + relation attribution to fix — i.e., exactly the
expensive structured-engineering the pre-feasibility gate exists to avoid buying blindly.

## 9. Why This Confirms the Freeze Rather Than Opens a Path

The cheapest version of the spec-lattice idea measures ~40 points BELOW its usability gate, with
failure mass concentrated in structural problems (tables, co-reference) rather than edge noise.
No plausible incremental patch closes 61%→95% while keeping extraction deterministic and cheap:
each known remedy re-introduces PDF-layout parsing or heuristic column inference whose own
feasibility would need another gated cycle. The honest reading: the missing evidence asset is
real but NOT cheaply reachable from today's chunk store.

Additionally — and decisively for scope control — even a PERFECT lattice validator attacks at
most the fabricated-spec subset of FAs (≈5–6 of 9): it does not address the G120-pair pattern,
and V3.79/V3.80-C already proved the remaining slices have no separating signal. Maximum
theoretical recovery (~7pp accuracy) sits behind the largest engineering risk measured so far.
