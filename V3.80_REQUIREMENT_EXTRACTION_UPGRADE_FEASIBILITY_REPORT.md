## 38. Engineering Decision

V3.80-C closes the loop opened by V3.79 with a clean negative result on the decisive question.
The extractor upgrade achieved everything achievable WITHOUT new semantics: it converted nearly
every ambiguous query into a typed, verifiable-shape claim (11/13) with zero structural errors
and millisecond cost — and the merged system got WORSE (precision down, kills up, five unsafe
supports created). The definitive lesson: AMBIGUOUS-as-safe-reject is not a coverage bug to be
eradicated; it is the honest verdict for claims whose verification needs "a documented fixed
specification exists" — a quantified statement about corpus content that neither token coverage
nor sentence-local co-occurrence approximates. Closing the 20-case split therefore requires an
asset that does not exist anywhere in the repo today (a spec-existence/fact lattice validated on
independent data), i.e., NEW architecture with its own multi-phase risk — exactly the path §42's
STOP is designed to force us to take deliberately rather than by pattern accretion. Per instruction
§55/56, nothing beyond shadow tooling is proposed; runtime remains frozen at the V3.77 boundary.
