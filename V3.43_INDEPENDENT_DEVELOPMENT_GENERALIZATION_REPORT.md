## 3. Benchmark design

`v343-evidence-sufficiency-independent-dev-v1` contains exactly 60 unique English queries:
30 ANSWER and 30 ABSTAIN. Each required case has a 5/5 label split.

| Case | Queries | ANSWER | ABSTAIN |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Identity-compatible evidence recovery | 10 | 5 | 5 |
| Technical synonym | 10 | 5 | 5 |
| Abbreviation | 10 | 5 | 5 |
| Parameter table relation | 10 | 5 | 5 |
| Section inherited evidence | 10 | 5 | 5 |
| Cross-reference evidence | 10 | 5 | 5 |

The corpus uses two new official English industrial PDFs:

1. Turck, *TBEN-S2-4IOL IO-Link Master Module - Instructions for Use*, V03.00,
   2025-04, 196 pages. Official URL: `https://www.turck.de/attachment/D301369.pdf`.
   SHA256: `1dc130b8900be1d0aef762560c519d2c12b164c19ee3f9c71b2097800ad0ee57`.
2. SICK, *Operating instructions IO-Link Master IOLG2EC-03208R01 - EtherCAT*, document
   8018499.YV31, 52 pages. Official URL:
   `https://cdn.sick.com/media/docs/4/74/174/operating_instructions_iolg2ec_03208r01_ethercat_en_im0061174.pdf`.
   SHA256: `ff63c205f6a3b8b6da69dae37898b724bc529e572a386c873dcaee24ca511046`.

Both documents passed official-domain, hash, physical page count, English-text, rendered-page,
and production-ingestion checks. Frozen production ingestion generated 641 non-empty chunks:
475 Turck and 166 SICK.

The independence audit compared document identifiers, official URLs, and SHA256 values against
historical manifests. It rejected a proposed WAGO document because that exact PDF belonged to
Corpus H. The selected Turck and SICK documents had zero prior-document overlap. The benchmark
loaded no A-H, J, K, or historical sealed query/plaintext data. All 60 normalized query hashes
were compared with V3.42 and had zero overlap.

All ANSWER annotations bind a production chunk and record target, relation, attribute, and
value/action. All ABSTAIN rows are hard negatives covering:

- same family, wrong parameter;
- same manufacturer, wrong model;
- same value, wrong attribute;
- correct identity, insufficient evidence.

The dataset and candidate hash were frozen before prediction. Baseline and candidate each have
`run_count: 1`; the candidate replayed the exact five production hybrid candidates selected by
the baseline. An initial ingestion process ended before writing artifacts, and a later successful
ingestion process encountered a console-encoding error only after atomically writing the corpus
artifacts. Neither event involved predictions or changed the frozen dataset.

## 4. Implementation

V3.43 adds no reasoning candidate and changes no V3.42 code. It evaluates the unchanged
`evidence-v342-sufficiency-candidate` as required by independent generalization.

The public V3.43 module provides only:

- the private benchmark schema and distribution checks;
- document and query independence gates;
- the frozen candidate version/hash gate;
- the unchanged V3.42 metric and acceptance policy;
- additional one-shot and runtime-integrity requirements.

The candidate decision boundary therefore remains exactly V3.42: V3.41 runs first; only an
identity-COMPATIBLE VERIFICATION refusal may be reconsidered; a single chunk must bind target,
relation, attribute, and value/action; and only a supported relation above the frozen confidence
floor may answer.
