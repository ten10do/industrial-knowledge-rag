## 3. Benchmark design

The sealed gate contains 60 identity-focused manual verification queries:

- 30 ANSWER and 30 ABSTAIN.
- 60/60 L5 and 60/60 HIGH confidence.
- Coverage includes family/model, controller/module, option/accessory,
  firmware/version, same-name component, parameter ownership, and
  cross-document identity.
- All 30 ABSTAIN cases are hard identity near misses.
- Safe positives exercise explicit universal document scope, directional
  child variants, submodule variants, and a documented version floor.
- Unsafe negatives preserve the technical relation while changing the model,
  module, option, firmware, or parameter owner.

One audited production-chunk excerpt was supplied as the frozen evidence
candidate for each query. This isolates Evidence-layer identity behavior from
retrieval-ranking variance. It does not replace the Evidence decision: both
versions run through their identity boundary and then delegate compatible or
unknown cases to the formal mixed-evidence runtime.

## 8. Failure taxonomy

The candidate has no false answers and 20 false refusals:

| Failure class | Count | Attribution |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `IDENTITY_FALSE_REFUSAL` | 5 | Boundary stopped the query before mixed Evidence; four broader-scope cases and one firmware case remained over-strict. |
| `EXISTING_EVIDENCE_FALSE_REFUSAL` | 11 | Identity was compatible, but the unchanged rule/hard-gate path still abstained. |
| `NLI_FALSE_REFUSAL` | 4 | Identity was compatible, but the selectively triggered frozen NLI path did not yield an answer. |

Among the 20 failed positives, the boundary reason distribution was eight
`MODEL_VARIANT_DESCENDANT`, four `DOCUMENT_SCOPE_COVERS_MODEL`, four
`BROADER_EVIDENCE_SCOPE`, three `MODULE_VARIANT_DESCENDANT`, and one
`FIRMWARE_MISMATCH`. This means V3.36 often recognized a safe identity
relation but could not preserve utility through the unchanged downstream
Evidence contract. The result is therefore a mixed residual: five boundary
over-refusals and 15 downstream Evidence/NLI refusals.

The ten successful positives consisted of eight model-variant descendants and
two explicit document-scope coverage cases. Safety behavior remained intact
for sibling models, modules, options, firmware, and parameter ownership.
