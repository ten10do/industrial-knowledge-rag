# V3.35 Sealed Identity Validation Gate Report

## Decision

**SEALED_IDENTITY_FAIL**

The identity-aware candidate materially reduced false answers, but it failed the
pre-registered false-refusal constraint. False Answer fell by **40.00 percentage
points** (46.67% to 6.67%), while False Refusal rose by **13.33 percentage
points** (16.67% to 30.00%). The allowed increase was at most 5 percentage
points. The gate was run once and was not tuned or replayed.

## 2. Independence and leakage audit

The Corpus L builder checked exact official URL, PDF SHA-256, and normalized
title against the accumulated A-H/J forbidden manifests and Corpus K. No
overlap was found. All six Corpus L manufacturers are distinct within this
gate. Manufacturer-disjointness from every historical corpus was not a gate
claim; document-disjointness was the binding requirement and passed.

The benchmark was authored only from Corpus L production chunks. Each evidence
excerpt was checked as a normalized verbatim substring of its recorded
production chunk before freeze. Historical sealed plaintext, D/E/H/J
plaintext, V3.34 DEV queries, and historical results were not loaded during
benchmark construction. No old query was copied or replayed.

Private PDFs, chunks, queries, annotations, ledgers, and results remain under
the gitignored `backend/evaluation/benchmark_private/` tree and are not part of
the public change set.

## 3. Benchmark design

The gate contains 60 manual-first verification queries:

- 30 ANSWER and 30 ABSTAIN.
- 30 L4 positives and 30 L5 hard negatives; L4-L5 coverage is 100%.
- 60/60 HIGH-confidence annotations.
- Ten queries in each of six identity slices: family/model,
  module/controller, option/accessory, firmware/version, same-name
  different-product, and parameter scope.
- Each positive/negative pair uses an independently reviewed Corpus L claim;
  negatives preserve the technical relation while changing only the identity
  boundary under test.

The evaluation used a frozen manual-evidence bundle with one audited production
chunk excerpt per query. This deliberately removes retrieval variance from an
Evidence-layer validation. Candidate metadata consists of production chunk
metadata plus identity fields already declared in the frozen Corpus L source
manifest. Retrieval, parser, and reranker implementations were not changed.

## 8. Failure taxonomy

Baseline failures:

- 14 `BASELINE_IDENTITY_FALSE_ANSWER`.
- 5 `EXISTING_EVIDENCE_FALSE_REFUSAL`, all in the firmware/version slice.

Candidate failures:

- 4 `IDENTITY_FALSE_REFUSAL` caused by the boundary returning
  `NO_COMPATIBLE_IDENTITY_CLAIM` / `BROADER_EVIDENCE_SCOPE` on permitted
  family-level positives.
- 5 inherited `EXISTING_EVIDENCE_FALSE_REFUSAL` in firmware/version; these
  already failed under the baseline and were not introduced by the identity
  boundary.
- 2 residual identity false answers: one family/model case and one parameter
  case. Both returned `IDENTITY_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN` and delegated to the existing
  rule path.

Boundary outcomes were 29 INCOMPATIBLE, 16 UNKNOWN, and 15 COMPATIBLE. The
candidate successfully blocked the dominant module/controller,
option/accessory, and sibling-product leakage. Its main sealed failure is not
insufficient FA reduction; it is incorrect rejection of answerable family
scope, which violates the recall-preservation objective.
