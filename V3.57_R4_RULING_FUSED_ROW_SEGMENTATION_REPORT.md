## 21. NEXT_PHASE → STRUCTURE_PRODUCER_RESPONSIBILITY_REASSESSMENT

Mechanism proof: 66/139 declines are value texts split across two adjacent
rows — recoverable ONLY by merging occupied-column continuations. But:

1. §17 prohibits "no-line → merge", and the ruling signal's separation
   power (46% vs 31%) is too weak to safely authorize occupied-column
   merges (would misclassify ~31% of true wraps as merges and vice versa);
2. a semantic substring-tolerant binder would move correctness into fuzzy
   matching, violating the producer/exact-contract boundary established in
   V3.51-V3.55.

Both remaining paths require an owner-level architecture decision:
(a) accept substring/prefix binding with strengthened downstream Evidence
    verification (contract boundary change), or
(b) introduce a structure-capture-only library (e.g., PyMuPDF) providing
    decoded char-level coordinates, enabling correct wrap detection without
    touching Evidence semantics, or
(c) freeze the structured-producer track at PARTIAL and re-plan V3.58+
    around flat-text evidence with table-region metadata only.

Heuristic refinement is STOPPED here per §29. No further producer patches
will be attempted without that decision.
