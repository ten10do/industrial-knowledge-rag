### 3.3 Root cause (fact, with evidence)

On Rockwell and Mitsubishi manuals the table cell TEXT is only decodable
through font ToUnicode/CMap machinery that pypdf applies inside its own
aggregated extraction. Two reader strategies were implemented and measured:

- **Operation-level walker**: correct per-show-op POSITIONS and geometry,
  but raw encoded bytes as strings (no CMap application) → mojibake texts
  that can never match ground truth.
- **pypdf visitor callbacks**: correctly DECODED strings, but aggregated
  granularity (whole rows per callback on Td-driven producers) and broken
  memoized positions on same-line advances → cannot split rows into cells.

An adaptive quality-based choice between them therefore always loses one
capability: either decoded-but-unsegmentable text or segmentable-but-
undecodable text. Cell-level decoded reconstruction on these manuals
requires char-map-aware decoding at operation level (deep integration with
`pypdf._cmap`, version-pinned internals) or a second parsing library
(e.g., pdfminer.six) — both are architecture/resource decisions outside
this phase's bounded-refinement budget.

Danfoss/WEG pages (direct encoding) DID produce readable cells end-to-end,
confirming the pipeline logic itself is sound; the blocker is specifically
CMap-encoded XObject text.

## 6. Decision options (requires owner input)

1. **Approve a pinned secondary parser dependency** (pdfminer.six or
   PyMuPDF) for CMap-correct char-level text+coordinates, then re-attempt
   V3.57 with unchanged gates. Strongest expected payoff; adds dependency
   governance burden.
2. **Invest in pypdf-internal CMap integration** (`pypdf._cmap`,
   version-pinned, wrapped behind our own interface). No new dependency,
   higher maintenance risk against pypdf upgrades.
3. **Restrict future corpora to directly-encoded manuals** (like Danfoss/
   WEG style) — weakens generality; not recommended as the primary path.
4. **Abandon the structured-producer track** (section 33) — premature:
   V3.56 feasibility and the synthetic slice show contract logic and
   binding safety are sound; the blocker is one well-characterized
   toolchain limitation.

Recommendation: option 1 or 2, decided by the project owner; V3.58+ remain
locked until V3.57 reaches its gates.
