## 37. Engineering Decision

The aligned V3.77 benchmark did its job: it converted a vague suspicion into a precise,
mirror-validated causal chain. The system's unsafe-answer channel is real (7/9 FAs through one
unconditional accept branch, accepting content up to 2.86× past the similarity bound), but the
channel cannot be closed by gating logic alone — the same branch legitimately carries 13 correct
answers that sit even further out than most FAs, because long-tail wording is normal for industrial
manuals while "junk-supported" coverage is indistinguishable to every predicate the runtime computes
today. Closing it safely requires a NEW validated evidence-quality signal (e.g., claim-level
support validation between required claim and retrieved passage text) developed and generalized in
dedicated phases — not a threshold tweak, not a boundary flip. V3.78 therefore stops honestly at
NOT_JUSTIFIED rather than shipping an unsafe "safety improvement".
