## Engineering Decision

V3.74's premise that the 19 FR are caused by a coverage threshold is refuted. The full audit shows a multi-mechanism split with no single coverage predicate reaching 60%, and only 2 coverage-bound FR. The largest remaining mechanism is identity (models absent from the benchmark corpus), followed by a harness fidelity gap (vector score not wired, so the 13.23 gate is never evaluated). Threshold calibration is low leverage and is not justified.
